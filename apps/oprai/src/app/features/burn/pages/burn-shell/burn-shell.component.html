How does it work?

🔓
Close Empty Accounts

Every SPL token account locks ~0.002 SOL in rent. When you close an account with zero balance, that SOL is returned to your wallet. This is completely safe and reversible — your wallet will automatically re-create the account the next time you receive that token.

🔥
Burn Tokens

Burning destroys tokens permanently by sending them to the TokenkegQfeZyiNwAJb… burn address. This cannot be undone. Only burn tokens you no longer want. You can optionally close the account at the same time to also reclaim the rent SOL.

💡
Why do I have so many accounts?

Every time you interact with a token for the first time (swap, airdrop, transfer), Solana creates a token account for it. Over time these accumulate. Closing empty ones is a free way to clean up your wallet and recover locked SOL.

💰
How much SOL can I recover?

Each empty account returns approximately 0.00203928 SOL. If you have 10 empty accounts, you can recover ~0.02 SOL. The exact amount shown is the actual rent locked in each account.
