# DeFi Core Glossary

## AMM (Automated Market Maker)
A type of decentralized exchange (DEX) that uses a mathematical formula to price tokens instead of an order book. Liquidity providers (LPs) deposit token pairs into pools, and the AMM algorithm (e.g., x*y=k for Uniswap/Orca classic) maintains the price. Common variants on Solana: constant product AMM (Orca Classic, Raydium v2), CLMM concentrated liquidity (Orca Whirlpools, Raydium CLMM), DLMM (Meteora).

## APY (Annual Percentage Yield)
The annualized return on an investment, accounting for compounding. In DeFi, APY = (1 + r/n)^n - 1 where r is the annual interest rate and n is compounding periods per year. Differs from APR (Annual Percentage Rate), which does not compound.

## CLMM (Concentrated Liquidity Market Maker)
An AMM where liquidity providers specify a price range for their capital rather than providing liquidity across the entire price curve. Capital earns fees only when price is within the specified range, allowing higher capital efficiency at the cost of requiring active management. Popularized by Uniswap v3; on Solana: Orca Whirlpools, Raydium CLMM.

## Collateral Ratio
The ratio of collateral value to borrowed value in a lending position. A collateral ratio of 150% means $150 of collateral secures $100 of debt. Protocols set a minimum collateral ratio (liquidation threshold) below which the position is liquidated.

## DLMM (Dynamic Liquidity Market Maker)
Meteora's liquidity design where liquidity is distributed across discrete price bins rather than a continuous curve. Each bin has a fixed price, and traders only interact with the active bin. Enables ultra-low slippage for on-curve trades and better capital efficiency than standard CLMM.

## Flash Loan
A loan that is borrowed and repaid within the same transaction. If the transaction fails (e.g., repayment not included), the entire transaction reverts. Used for arbitrage, collateral swaps, and liquidations without requiring upfront capital.

## Health Factor
A metric used by lending protocols (Kamino, Solend) to measure the safety of a borrowing position. Calculated as (weighted collateral value) / (borrowed value). A health factor below 1.0 triggers liquidation. Higher is safer.

## Impermanent Loss (IL)
The temporary loss in value experienced by liquidity providers compared to simply holding the tokens. Occurs when the price ratio of the deposited token pair diverges from the ratio at deposit time. Called "impermanent" because the loss is only realized upon withdrawal; if prices return to original ratio, the loss disappears.

## JLP (Jupiter Liquidity Provider)
The liquidity pool token for Jupiter's perpetuals exchange. JLP holders act as the counterparty to all perpetual traders on Jupiter. JLP earns 75% of all fees generated by the perpetuals platform (opening/closing fees, borrow fees, liquidation fees). JLP is a yield-bearing basket of USDC, SOL, ETH, BTC, and wBTC.

## LTV (Loan-to-Value)
The ratio of borrowed amount to collateral value. A 70% LTV means you can borrow 70 cents for every $1 of collateral deposited. Lower LTV = more collateral required. Protocols set maximum LTV and liquidation LTV thresholds.

## MEV (Maximal Extractable Value)
Profit that validators or searchers can extract by reordering, inserting, or censoring transactions within blocks. On Solana, MEV primarily occurs through arbitrage and liquidation bots. Jito's block engine captures MEV tips and distributes them to validators and stakers.

## PnL (Profit and Loss)
Net profit or loss from a trading or investment position. Calculated as: current value - entry cost (for long positions). In DeFi, includes all fees paid and rewards earned.

## Slippage
The difference between the expected price of a trade and the actual executed price. Caused by market movement during transaction processing and by the AMM's price impact (larger trades move the price more). Users set a slippage tolerance to prevent execution at worse-than-expected prices.

## TVL (Total Value Locked)
The aggregate value of assets deposited in a DeFi protocol. Commonly used as a measure of protocol adoption and health. DeFiLlama tracks TVL across chains and protocols.

## Yield Farming
The practice of deploying capital across multiple DeFi protocols to maximize yield. Involves providing liquidity, lending, borrowing, and staking in combination. Risks include smart contract bugs, impermanent loss, and protocol token depreciation.
