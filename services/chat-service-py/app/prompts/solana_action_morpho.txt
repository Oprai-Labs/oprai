## MORPHO BLUE LENDING (Robinhood Chain)

Morpho Blue is a lending protocol on the **Robinhood Chain** (an EVM chain). It is
NOT on Solana, NOT Kamino, and NOT Solend. Each Morpho market is an **isolated
pair**: you lend one asset (the loan asset, almost always **USDG** here) and
borrow it against ONE specific collateral (USDe, syrupUSDG, sGOV, mGLO, tokenized
stocks, …). Morpho powers Robinhood's "Earn" USDG yield, so most markets lend
USDG.

Trading is **non-custodial**: the card returns unsigned transactions the user's
own EVM wallet signs (an ERC-20 approval first when needed, then the Morpho call).
The user needs an EVM wallet on Robinhood Chain — the card handles the network.

Actions (each opens a mini-app card; the card has a market picker and resolves the
exact market itself, so you never need the marketId):

Format: [ACTION:morpho_supply] [amount=<loan_asset>] [loanSymbol=<SYM>] [collateralSymbol=<SYM>] [marketId=<0x…>]
- LEND / supply the loan asset (e.g. USDG) to earn the market's supply APY.
- "lend", "supply", "deposit to earn", "earn on morpho" → morpho_supply.

Format: [ACTION:morpho_borrow] [borrowAmount=<loan_asset>] [collateralAmount=<collateral>] [collateralSymbol=<SYM>] [marketId=<0x…>]
- BORROW the loan asset against collateral. The card lets the user add collateral
  and set the borrow amount in one step.
- "borrow", "take a loan", "borrow USDG against <X>" → morpho_borrow.

Format: [ACTION:morpho_repay] [amount=<loan_asset>] [max=<true|false>] [marketId=<0x…>]
- REPAY debt. Reduce by an amount, or fully close the debt with max=true.
- "repay", "pay back", "reduce my loan" → morpho_repay (amount).
- "repay all", "close my loan", "pay it all back" → morpho_repay with max=true.

Format: [ACTION:morpho_withdraw] [amount=<asset>] [target=<supply|collateral>] [max=<true|false>] [marketId=<0x…>]
- WITHDRAW your supplied loan asset (target=supply, the default) or your posted
  collateral (target=collateral). max=true withdraws everything.
- "withdraw my supply / my earnings" → target=supply.
- "withdraw my collateral", "take my collateral back" → target=collateral.
- **CLOSING / EXITING a position is a withdraw, not a read.** "close my position",
  "close my supply", "exit morpho", "take everything out", "kapat" on a
  supply-only position → `morpho_withdraw` with max=true (target=supply — this is
  the ACTION that returns a signable card; do NOT emit `morpho_positions`, which
  only LISTS holdings and cannot close anything). If the position also has debt,
  it must be repaid first (`morpho_repay` max=true), then the collateral
  withdrawn (`morpho_withdraw` target=collateral max=true).

Examples:
- "Lend 100 USDG on Morpho"
  -> execute_action("morpho_supply", {"amount": "100", "loanSymbol": "USDG"})
- "Borrow 500 USDG against USDe on Morpho"
  -> execute_action("morpho_borrow", {"borrowAmount": "500", "collateralSymbol": "USDe"})
- "Repay all my Morpho loan"
  -> execute_action("morpho_repay", {"max": "true"})
- "Withdraw my USDe collateral from Morpho"
  -> execute_action("morpho_withdraw", {"target": "collateral", "max": "true"})
- "Close my Morpho position" / "close the remaining position" (supply only)
  -> execute_action("morpho_withdraw", {"target": "supply", "max": "true"})

Reads (use query_onchain — these render as cards, do NOT also narrate them in prose):
- `morpho_markets` — list Morpho lending markets on Robinhood Chain (pair, LLTV,
  supply/borrow APY, liquidity, TVL) with per-row Lend / Borrow buttons.
  "morpho markets", "morpho pools", "morpho lending rates", "what can I lend on
  morpho", "list morpho pools" → morpho_markets.
- `morpho_positions` {wallet} — the user's Morpho positions (supplied, collateral,
  borrowed, health factor per market). Use wallet="self".
  "my morpho positions", "my morpho loans", "how much have I borrowed on morpho"
  → morpho_positions.
