> **HOW TO READ THESE EXAMPLES**: The notation `[ACTION:X] param=value` documents the `action_type` and parameter names to pass via `execute_action("X", {"param": "value"})`. **Never emit `[ACTION:...]` text in your response** — always call the `execute_action` function tool instead.

## STAKING PROTOCOLS

### MARINADE FINANCE ACTIONS

#### MARINADE_STAKE — Stake SOL for mSOL on Marinade
Format: [ACTION:marinade_stake] amount=<amount>

Examples:
- "Stake 10 SOL on Marinade"
  -> [ACTION:marinade_stake] amount=10
- "Marinade ile stake et"
  -> [ACTION:marinade_stake] amount=1

#### MARINADE_UNSTAKE — Instant unstake mSOL to SOL (fee ~0.3%)
Format: [ACTION:marinade_unstake] amount=<amount>

Examples:
- "Instant unstake my mSOL"
  -> [ACTION:marinade_unstake] amount=all
- "mSOL'u SOL'a çevir"
  -> [ACTION:marinade_unstake] amount=5

#### MARINADE_DELAYED_UNSTAKE — Delayed unstake mSOL (no fee, ~2 epoch wait)
Format: [ACTION:marinade_delayed_unstake] amount=<amount>

Examples:
- "Delayed unstake my mSOL"
  -> [ACTION:marinade_delayed_unstake] amount=all
- "Gecikmeli unstake"
  -> [ACTION:marinade_delayed_unstake] amount=10

### JITO FINANCE ACTIONS

#### JITO_STAKE — Stake SOL for jitoSOL (MEV-enhanced)
Format: [ACTION:jito_stake] amount=<amount>

Examples:
- "Stake on Jito"
  -> [ACTION:jito_stake] amount=10
- "Jito ile stake et"
  -> [ACTION:jito_stake] amount=5

#### JITO_UNSTAKE — Unstake jitoSOL to SOL
Format: [ACTION:jito_unstake] amount=<amount>

Examples:
- "Unstake from Jito"
  -> [ACTION:jito_unstake] amount=all

#### JITO_TIP — Send a tip to Jito validators
Format: [ACTION:jito_tip] amount=<amount>

Examples:
- "Send 0.01 SOL tip to Jito"
  -> [ACTION:jito_tip] amount=0.01

#### JITO_BUNDLE — Create a Jito bundle (multiple transactions atomically)
Format: [ACTION:jito_bundle] transactions=<base64_tx_1,base64_tx_2,...>

(Note: Advanced feature for advanced users)

#### JITO_BUNDLE_STATUS — Check status of a Jito bundle
Format: [ACTION:jito_bundle_status] bundleId=<bundle_id>

(Note: This is a data query)
